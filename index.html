Daily history

Live data via the public Huxley2 wrapper around National Rail's real-time Darwin departure boards. A scheduled job polls a set of major SWR hub stations every 10 minutes and counts each unique train service once — this reflects services passing through those stations, not a literal count of every train on the entire network (no public API exposes that directly).
